### Klein ablation pilot

The experiment documented in `reports/klein-ablation-pilot/` is an initial pilot of our experiment. We used AI throughout its setup, execution, and analysis. This included designing the ablation comparisons, setting up the local environment, writing and debugging the evaluation code, running the experiment, checking the results, and drafting the findings.

- **Experiment setup:** AI helped define the eight configurations comparing full sampling, reduced-step sampling, fixed feature reuse, Taylor forecasting, and adaptive verification. It also helped prepare the five prompts, generation settings, and reproducibility metadata.
- **Implementation and execution:** AI wrote and revised the experiment runner and supporting scripts, including `scripts/evaluate-klein.py`, `scripts/score-evaluation.py`, and `scripts/collect-macmon.py`. It helped configure dependencies, troubleshoot execution, generate the 40 images, and collect timing, memory, and CPU+GPU power measurements.
- **Validation:** AI wrote tests and validation code to check model equivalence, trial coverage, image identity, timing totals, computation counts, speedup calculations, and energy integration. These checks were run against the experiment outputs.
- **Analysis and documentation:** AI produced the quality-score summaries, plots, image comparison grids, and report artifacts using `scripts/report-evaluation.py`. It also drafted the pilot's `README.md` and `findings.md`, including the interpretation of the results, limitations, and proposed next experiments.

The images were generated by the Klein model, and the numerical results came from local execution and scoring. AI also wrote much of the code used to measure and validate those results, so the automated checks should not be treated as an independent human review.

### Samplers and guidance

We chose which sampling methods to add to the codebase: DPM-Solver++ 2M and UniPC as higher-order samplers, and limited-interval guidance and adaptive guidance as guidance policies. We also ran the sampler and guidance experiments on our benchmark prompts. Claude Code wrote the implementation of these methods in the following files:

- `feature_reuse/samplers.py`: DPM-Solver++ 2M and UniPC samplers for flow matching
- `feature_reuse/guidance.py`: limited-interval and adaptive guidance policies
- `scripts/diagnose-guidance.py` and `scripts/summarize-guidance.py`: guidance diagnostics and threshold selection
- `tests/test_samplers.py` and `tests/test_guidance.py`
- The original "Samplers and guidance" section of `README.md`, now preserved in `PLAN.md`

The unit tests check the samplers' accuracy against Euler and their behavior at the first and last steps. The samplers were also compared with the diffusers implementations outside the test suite, and `scripts/check-klein-equivalence.py` confirms on the real model that the default settings reproduce plain CFG and MFLUX's Euler sampler.
